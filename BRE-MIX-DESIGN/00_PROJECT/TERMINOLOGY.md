# Terminology

> Consistent engineering terminology used throughout this project.

| Term | Definition | Symbol | Unit |
|------|-----------|--------|------|
| Characteristic strength | The value of compressive strength below which no more than a specified percentage of test results may fall | f_ck (or f_c) | N/mm² |
| Target mean strength | The mean compressive strength required to ensure the characteristic strength is achieved statistically | f_m | N/mm² |
| Margin | The difference between target mean strength and characteristic strength | M | N/mm² |
| Standard deviation | A measure of the variability of concrete strength test results | s | N/mm² |
| Proportion defective | The permitted percentage of test results falling below the characteristic strength | - | % |
| k factor | A statistical constant related to the proportion defective, derived from the normal distribution | k | dimensionless |
| Free water/cement ratio | The ratio of the mass of free water to the mass of cement in a concrete mix | w/c | dimensionless |
| Free water content | The mass of water per cubic metre of concrete available for cement hydration and workability (excluding water absorbed by aggregates) | W | kg/m³ |
| Cement content | The mass of cement per cubic metre of concrete | C | kg/m³ |
| Total aggregate content | The total mass of aggregates (fine + coarse) per cubic metre of concrete, on an SSD basis | A_total | kg/m³ |
| Fine aggregate | Aggregate passing a specified sieve size (typically ≤ 4 mm or ≤ 5 mm) | FA | kg/m³ |
| Coarse aggregate | Aggregate retained on a specified sieve size | CA | kg/m³ |
| Maximum aggregate size | The nominal maximum size of aggregate particle | d_max | mm |
| Slump | A measure of workability — the vertical subsidence of a cone of fresh concrete | - | mm |
| Vebe time | A measure of workability for very stiff mixes — the time for concrete to reform under vibration | - | seconds |
| Workability | The ease with which fresh concrete can be mixed, placed, compacted, and finished | - | (classified) |
| Relative density | The ratio of the mass of a material to the mass of an equal volume of water (also called specific gravity) | SG or RD | dimensionless |
| Saturated surface-dry (SSD) | The condition of aggregate where internal pores are filled with water but no free water exists on the particle surface | SSD | - |
| Oven-dry | The condition of aggregate after drying to constant mass at 105 ± 5°C | OD | - |
| Free moisture | The water present on the surface of aggregate particles beyond the SSD condition | - | % or kg |
| Absorption | The amount of water absorbed into aggregate pores from the oven-dry to SSD condition | - | % |
| Wet density | The density of fully compacted fresh concrete | D_wet | kg/m³ |
| Batch quantity | The quantity of each material required for a specific batch volume | - | kg |
| Percentage passing 600 µm | The percentage by mass of fine aggregate passing a 600-micron sieve — a key grading parameter in BRE Figure 6 | %600 | % |
| Portland cement | Cement conforming to BS EN 197-1, CEM I | OPC / PC | - |
| PFA | Pulverised-fuel ash (fly ash) — a supplementary cementitious material | PFA | - |
| GGBS | Ground granulated blastfurnace slag — a supplementary cementitious material | GGBS | - |
| Cement strength class | Classification of cement by minimum 28-day compressive strength (e.g., 42.5, 52.5) per BS EN 197-1 | - | N/mm² |
| Trial mix | A laboratory batch of concrete prepared to verify that the designed mix meets requirements | - | - |
