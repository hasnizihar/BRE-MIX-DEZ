## Unit Equivalences

```
1 N/mm² = 1 MPa = 1 MN/m²
1 kg/m³ = 1 g/L
```

---

## Conventions

1. **N/mm²** is used throughout (not MPa), consistent with the BRE publication and UK practice.
2. All mass quantities are per cubic metre of concrete unless specified as batch quantities.
3. Aggregate quantities are on an **SSD basis** in the design, corrected to **wet basis** for batching.
4. No unit conversions are required within the standard BRE method — all inputs and outputs use consistent SI/metric units.
